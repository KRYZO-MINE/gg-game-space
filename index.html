What GG Game Space is

GG Game Space keeps your game tools in one place. You add the games you play to the app, then bring up its tool panel while the game is running instead of switching between apps.

It is built for mobile players who want controls, display and device tools within reach during a session. It does not guarantee improvements to a game and does not replace the game's own settings.

Details on this site reflect the app's Google Play listing and the screenshots shown here. Feature availability, behaviour and results can vary by device, Android version and game.

How it works

  1. Add your games

    The app keeps an Added Games list. Add the games you play to it.

  2. Open a game

    Launch the game and bring up the GG Game Space panel over it, as shown in the screenshots.

  3. Pick a tool

    Choose a tool from the panel, such as Keymap or Resolution management.
