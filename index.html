About GG Game Space

GG Game Space keeps your game tools in one place. Add your games to the app, then bring up the tool panel without leaving the game.

It is built for mobile players who want controls, display and device tools within reach while they play.

Tools you can see in the app

The Google Play listing describes 100+ tools. These examples are visible in the app screenshots; availability and behavior can vary by game and device.

  • Keymap

    Control mapping, found in the tool panel.

  • Macro

    Macro tools, found in the tool panel.

  • Virtual Crosshair

    An on-screen crosshair shown over the game.

  • Device Info Monitor

    A readout of device information while you play.

  • Resolution Management

    Resolution settings, listed in the tool panel.

  • FPS Booster

    A frame-rate tool. Results depend on the game and device.

  • Volume and Brightness

    Slider controls for volume and screen brightness.

The panel, in the game

How it works

  1. Add your games

    The app keeps an Added Games list. Add the games you play to it.

  2. Open a game

    The tool panel appears over the game, as shown in the screenshots.

  3. Pick a tool

    Choose a tool from the panel, such as Keymap or Resolution management.

Install from the Google Play listing. Device setup can differ by manufacturer.
