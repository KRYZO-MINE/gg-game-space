GG Game Space features

GG Game Space is an Android game-tools app. The official Google Play listing describes more than 100 tools; the tools below are the ones visible in the app screenshots on this site. What each tool does, and how well it works, can depend on your device, Android version and the game you open.

Every tool is opened the same way: start the game from GG Game Space, then open the panel that appears over the game. The steps below describe that flow in more detail.

Controls and overlays

Keymap

Keymap maps on-screen game controls to the inputs you prefer. It is listed in the in-game tool panel.

How to use it

  1. Add and launch your game from GG Game Space so the panel can appear over it.
  2. Open the panel and choose Keymap.
  3. Place and assign the controls you want, then save the layout.
  4. Test the layout in a safe or practice area before relying on it in a match.

Notes and limitations: games and their publishers set their own rules about control mapping and automation. Use keymap only where the game allows it. Layouts may behave differently from game to game and after a game update.

Macro

The Macro tool records or builds a short sequence of actions that you can trigger from the panel.

How to use it

  1. Open the tool panel over your game and choose Macro.
  2. Create or record the sequence and save it.
  3. Trigger it from the panel and check the result in a low-risk part of the game.

Notes and limitations: macros can be treated as automation or cheating by some games. Check the game's rules before using this tool. Timing and results can vary by device performance and game update.

Virtual crosshair

The virtual crosshair draws a fixed on-screen crosshair over the game as a visual aiming reference.

How to use it

  1. Open the tool panel over your game and enable Virtual crosshair.
  2. Adjust its position or style if the app version offers options.

Notes and limitations: the crosshair is a visual aid only. It does not change how a game aims and is not a guarantee of improved accuracy or results.

Display and device tools

Device info monitor

A device information panel shows readouts about your phone while you play, so you can keep an eye on it without leaving the game.

How to use it: open the panel over your game and select Device info monitor. The exact metrics shown can change between app versions, so check the current app for the available values.

Notes and limitations: the monitor reports information; it does not by itself change performance.

Resolution management

Resolution management lets you change display resolution from the panel on setups that support it.

How to use it: open the panel over your game, choose Resolution management, and pick from the options your app version provides.

Notes and limitations: available options depend on the device, Android version and game. Changing resolution can affect image quality, and no specific frame rate or quality result is guaranteed.

FPS booster

A frame-rate related tool is listed in the panel. Results depend on your device hardware, software and the game's own limits.

How to use it: open the panel and select the FPS tool, then test it in your game.

Notes and limitations: this site and the Google Play listing provide no performance guarantees or benchmarks. A tool cannot exceed what your hardware and the game allow.

Volume and brightness

Quick sliders for volume and screen brightness are shown in the app screenshots, so you can adjust them without leaving the game.

How to use it: open the panel and use the volume or brightness slider.

What to check before relying on a tool

  • Confirm your device meets the Android requirements.
  • Follow the setup guide so the panel can appear over games.
  • Read the game's own rules before using keymap or macro tools.
  • If a tool does not appear or does not work, see troubleshooting.

Last reviewed: 25 October 2026. Tool details are based on the app's Google Play listing and the screenshots shown on this site; they can change between app versions and devices.
