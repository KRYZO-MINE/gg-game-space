Download GG Game Space

Get GG Game Space from its official Google Play listing. Google Play is the only download source linked from this website. Review the listing name, developer information and device availability before installing.

Get it on Google Play

Requirements before you install

  • Platform: Android phone or tablet.
  • Android version: Android 11 or later (developer-stated). Confirm availability for your device on the Play listing.
  • Root: not required.
  • Storage and permissions: the Play listing is the current source for install size and permissions. See the setup guide for the settings the app may ask for.

What to expect

  1. Open the Google Play listing and check that the app name and developer match.
  2. Install and open GG Game Space.
  3. Add a game to the app's list, then launch it and open the in-game tool panel.
  4. Work through any setup prompts your device shows; the setup guide explains them.

Version information

This website does not publish version numbers of its own. Google Play always shows the current version, size and recent updates for your device, so treat the listing as the source of truth. For a record of what has changed and when, see the changelog.

Staying safe

  • Use the official Google Play destination rather than third-party APK mirrors.
  • Check reviews, developer details and the Data safety section on the listing.
  • Review the app's privacy information page for how public disclosures are handled here.
  • If something does not work after installing, see troubleshooting.
