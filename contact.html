What to include when you contact support

Clear details help resolve an issue faster. Please include:

  • Your phone model and Android version.
  • The GG Game Space app version (shown on the Google Play listing and in your device's app info).
  • The game you were using, and the tool involved (for example keymap or virtual crosshair).
  • What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is visual.

Only send the information needed to identify and fix your issue. Support can only speak to GG Game Space and its website, not to third-party games.
