GG Game Space compatibility and requirements

Check these requirements before installing. Device makers customise Android, and game publishers can limit overlays or automation tools, so individual features may behave differently even on otherwise compatible devices.

How to read these status labels

  • Confirmed — stated in the app's public Google Play listing or by the developer.
  • Developer-stated / Not yet verified — provided by the developer or not independently tested here; treat it as a starting point, not a guarantee.
  • See guide — involves a device setting that needs care; follow the linked guide.

Why compatibility can vary

Android version, device manufacturer settings, screen layout, game updates and each game's own policy can all affect an in-game panel and its tools. A tool shown in the screenshots is not a promise that it works in every game. FPS, resolution, memory and other performance-related results depend on hardware and software and are not guaranteed.

Tested, reported, and not yet verified

To keep this page honest, we only label something “tested” when it has actually been tested here. Right now:

  • Tested: the Android platform and in-game panel flow shown in the app screenshots on this site.
  • Reported by users: not yet published — user reports will be added here once there are enough of them to be useful.
  • Not yet verified: specific phone models, specific game titles, and any performance results.

Help us verify your setup

If you have GG Game Space working (or not working) on a specific device, we'd like to know. When you report, include your phone model, Android version, app version, the game you tested and what happened.
