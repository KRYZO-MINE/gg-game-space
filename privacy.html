Privacy Information

Updated October 24, 2026. This page summarizes the website and the app's public Google Play disclosures. Store disclosures are supplied by the developer and are not an independent technical audit.

Publisher and contact

The Google Play listing names the developer as Shenzhen Leland Ltd. For questions or privacy requests through this website, email sahil@ggmousepro.com or use the GG Mouse Pro contact page.

GG Game Space app: published data disclosures

On the Google Play Data safety page, the developer currently declares that the app shares no data with third parties and collects no data. The same page says data is not encrypted and that users can request deletion. These are developer-provided statements and may change.

The privacy policy linked from the app's Google Play listing is dated May 4, 2025. It describes personal and usage data, accounts, device and diagnostic information, and possible sharing with service providers, affiliates, business partners, and other users. However, that linked policy identifies the app as “GG Mouse Pro 2” and the company as “Shenzhen Gamwing LTD,” rather than the Google Play listing's “GG Game Space” and “Shenzhen Leland Ltd.” This inconsistency means the linked policy cannot be confidently attributed to this app from the public information alone.

Read the privacy policy linked by the Google Play listing and contact the developer for clarification. Until the disclosures are reconciled by the publisher, this page does not claim that the app collects no data.

This website

This site consists of static pages and does not provide accounts, forms, or comments. Its pages do not include advertising or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address and requested page, to deliver and secure the site.

Requests and questions

To ask about app data or request deletion, email sahil@ggmousepro.com or use the GG Mouse Pro contact page. Include only the information needed to identify your request. The developer's response and any applicable legal rights depend on the app's actual data practices and the laws that apply to you.

Updates

Changes to this website notice will be posted on this page with a revised update date. The developer controls the app's data practices and its Google Play disclosures.
